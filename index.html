FAQ

Pricing questions, answered

Still unsure? Chat with us →

Can I change plans at any time?
−
Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time from your account settings. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
What happens after the free trial?
+
After your 7-day free trial, you'll be charged for the plan you selected. We'll remind you 2 days before the trial ends. Cancel anytime before then and you won't be charged.
Is there a student discount?
+
Yes! Students with a valid university email get 40% off Pro and Premium plans. Apply via our student verification page — discount is valid for up to 4 years.
Do you offer annual billing?
+
Yes — switch to yearly billing and save 35%. You'll be billed once per year and won't need to think about it again. Toggle the billing switch at the top of this page to see yearly prices.
What payment methods are accepted?
+
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and bank transfer for annual plans.
Can I share my account?
+
Pro allows up to 3 simultaneous streams on 1 account. Premium includes full family sharing with up to 5 independent profiles, each with their own watchlist and history.
Is there a money-back guarantee?
+
Absolutely. If you're not satisfied within the first 30 days of your paid plan, we'll refund you completely — no questions asked. Just contact our support team.
Do you offer business or team plans?
+
Yes! We offer custom enterprise plans for hotels, offices, and businesses. Contact our sales team at business@streamlive.com for a tailored quote.
